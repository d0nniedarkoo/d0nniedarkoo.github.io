Who
am I?

It all begins with an idea—but ideas never really go away, do they?

Christian O. Reyes is a Southern California filmmaker, photographer, editor, and California Baptist University graduate with a B.A. in Film Production.

His work moves between cinematic storytelling, analog texture, live-event production, and visual documentation. He has supported more than 100 university events for audiences ranging from 20 to 1,300 people.

Based in Jurupa Valley, California.

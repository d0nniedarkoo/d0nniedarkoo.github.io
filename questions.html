Questions / Answers

Frequently
asked.

What cameras do you enjoy using?

I enjoy what each camera changes about the way I see. Medium-format 120 film and Portra 400 remain favorites.

How do you develop film?

I use a C-41 process and carry that hands-on attention through scanning and the final edit.

What experience do you have?

School events, professional interviews, music venues, street photography, short films, editing, AV support, and large live productions.

What is your goal as a creator?

To make the things I can already feel but have not seen yet—and to keep improving the craft required to share them.

Where are you located?

Jurupa Valley, near Riverside, Corona, and Norco. I’m open to travel for the right project.

What work are you looking for?

Film crew, production support, editing, photography, event production, and collaborations with room for thoughtful storytelling.

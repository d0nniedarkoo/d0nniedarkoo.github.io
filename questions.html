Questions / Answers

Frequently
asked.

What cameras do you enjoy using?

Every camera offers something different. I especially enjoy medium-format 120 film and the color character of Portra 400.

How do you develop film?

I develop color film with a C-41 process and bring that hands-on attention into the final scan and edit.

What experience do you have?

My experience spans school events, professional interviews, music venues, street photography, poetry, short films, editing, and large-scale event support.

What is your goal as a creator?

To make the work I envision, improve with every project, and create images that people genuinely feel.

Where are you located?

I’m based in Jurupa Valley near Riverside, Corona, and Norco, and I’m open to travel for the right project.

What kind of work are you looking for?

Film crew, production support, editing, photography, event production, and creative collaborations with room for thoughtful storytelling.
